What it does

Everything you need on track

One app to time, read the engine and follow the line.

⏱️

F1-style timing

Purple/green/yellow sectors with history+ghost benchmark, theoretical lap and live delta. Finish line and splits on the map.

📡

OBD-II Telemetry

Connect an ELM327 adapter via Wi-Fi, Classic Bluetooth or BLE. Monitor RPM, throttle, temperatures, fuel, and MAF. Auto-priority for GPS/OBD speed.

🗺️

Native maps & routes

Standard, satellite, and hybrid maps. Draw routes with waypoints or freehand, record Tours, import GPX or Google Maps links.

📊

Advanced HUD & Widgets

Auto-merge adjacent cells. Insert minimap, sector map, and rally corner widgets, choosing F1, Rally, or Minimal styles.

🏁

Live Dashboard

Lap, delta, sectors, route, heading-up rotating minimap, rally corner indicators, analog or digital gauges, and a dynamic shift-light that adapts to the active vehicle's redline.

👻

Ghosts & Challenges

Beat the best ghost time (dot on the minimap). The purple benchmark adapts dynamically between your record and the ghost.

🎬

GoPro & Video Editor

Connect and control GoPro via BLE. Sync video with telemetry and overlay HUD, map and data (speed, G-force, sectors) directly onto footage with the built-in Pro Editor.

🛰️

External High-Frequency GPS

Support for external BLE GPS receivers (10–25 Hz: Garmin GLO, Dual XGPS, RaceBox). High-accuracy NMEA positioning that overrides the phone's built-in GPS.

✅

Preflight Check

Pre-session checklist: verifies GPS signal, OBD connection, camera readiness and vehicle setup before hitting the track. Zero surprises.

🔧

DTC Diagnostics

Read Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC) with direct fault descriptions. Targeted PID selection (e.g., ECU voltage, oil pressure).

🧪

OBD-II Simulator

Built-in software simulator generating mock telemetry data. Test your HUD and custom layouts without an ELM327 adapter or real car.

🚨

Personal speed traps

Place your targets on the map. Always-on GPS background monitoring and separate records for each vehicle.

🏎️

Vehicles & comparison

Built-in offline catalog. Manage multiple cars, save setups, and compare best laps and sectors on the same track.

🎵

Music control

Control play/pause and tracks for Spotify/YouTube Music (and others) without leaving the app. (Android exclusive).

📸

Social highlights

Create vertical stories with your photos/videos and colored sector times (purple/green/yellow) ready for social networks.

📹

Native video recordingBeta

Record track sessions with the phone's camera while syncing telemetry and lap timer in real time.

📂

Analysis & Export

Synchronized replay on map, speed-trace, and G-G diagram. Professional export in CSV, GPX, VBO, and NMEA (Premium).

🛡️

Secure diagnostic reports

Exportable debug logs for tech support. The app automatically obfuscates VIN, GPS coordinates, passwords and sensitive tokens.

Starting a lap

Start how you want

Three modes to start the timer perfectly.

MANUAL
Now

Press Start and the time begins instantly. The lap closes when you cross the finish line again.

LIGHTS
On green

Starting grid style: 5-light sequence with vibration feedback. The timer starts when lights go out. Also available as automatic rolling start above 5 km/h.

AUTOMATIC
At the line

Arms automatically near the line: the time starts the moment you cross the start line.
